Hey there! My name is [Srinath Obla](https://www.linkedin.com/in/srinathos/) and I’m an engineer. I currently live in Rochester, New York and am pursuing a master’s degree in Computer Science at the Rochester Institute of Technology. My broad interests lie in the fields of computer vision, machine learning, and data analysis – These fields are changing the landscape of our world and solving [hard](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohmajJTcpNk) [problems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tlThdr3O5Qo) at an impressive rate. 

I’m currently working on my master’s thesis where I’m enabling deep neural networks to efficiently process encrypted information _without decryption_. I’ve also worked on [tracking cars](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14gi-Bqq0pKQzDm7qWG79qqU_KoETDqyG/view?usp=sharing), [analyzing audio streams](https://github.com/srinathos/slightlyBetterRadio) and [reconstructing 3D objects for virtual reality](https://srinathos.github.io/3D-Object-Reconstruction/). During my time at RIT, I’ve graded and tutored graduate students in the field of [object oriented programming](http://spiegel.cs.rit.edu/~hpb/Lectures/2181/605/index.html) and [advanced computer vision](https://www.cs.rit.edu/~tbk/). Alongside my thesis, I’ve also worked as a Research Assistant at the Pervasive computing lab in RIT where I analyzed the properties of activation functions to construct deep neural networks that can process homomorphically encrypted data. Recently, I was awarded a scholarship for my research work by [TSO Logic, an Amazon Web Services company](https://tsologic.com/).

I’m always working on something I find interesting – software is and can transform everything we interact with. My posts [here](https://srinathos.github.io/) talk about my projects, research and issues (with my solutions, of course) I’ve faced over time. Feel free to [email](mailto:srinathobla@mail.rit.edu) me with any questions or suggestions!
