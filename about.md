Hey there! My name is Srinath. I currently live in Rochester, New York and am pursuing a master’s degree in Computer Science at the Rochester Institute of Technology. My research falls primarily in the field of Computer Vision and Artificial intelligence. I've worked with Matlab, OpenCV/C++, PCL and other tools such as Meshlab. While Java has been my language of choice, C++ is quickly growing on me and may perhaps soon take the crown.  

My first experience with computers started with my dad's Commodore 64. But my fascination grew only after I started programming in school. I started off experimenting with C, but the object-oriented paradigm was an appealing one and I switched to Java pretty quickly. By the time I had started my undergraduate degree in Computer Engineering, I had a pretty good hold of programming in Java. During my undergrad, I started working on Android solutions with the college's tech team. It was only during my final years at [SIESGST](http://www.siesgst.edu.in/) did I start working on Image Processing. 

During my term at RIT, I've worked with extracting fingerprints, tracking vehicles in parking lots and detecting anomalies. I'm currently a grader and TA for the Computer Vision course at RIT. I plan to record my projects and research using this blog and occasionally mention the troubles/solutions I've faced during their development. Feel free to drop me an email!
